# HEARTBEAT Gaza Partner Outreach Pack

**Prepared:** 5 October 2026  
**Public contact:** contact@heartbeatgaza.org  
**Website:** https://heartbeatgaza.org

## Purpose

This pack supports private, non-binding inquiries to organizations that may be
able to review, host, implement, fund, or introduce HEARTBEAT Gaza to an
appropriate partner.

HEARTBEAT Gaza is an independent concept initiated by Hasky. Hasky is not a
registered NGO or charity, is not collecting donations, and does not claim any
current endorsement or operational presence in Gaza.

## Private-Sector Contribution Model

Private companies are invited to contribute to clearly defined pilot costs,
not to send unrestricted money to Hasky. A contribution may take one of these
forms:

1. **Conditional funding pledge:** a written expression of interest that
   becomes payable only after an approved fiscal host and humanitarian
   implementation partner are in place.
2. **Restricted project grant:** funding paid to the approved partner for a
   named budget line such as technical validation, water, sanitation, energy,
   shelter materials, logistics or monitoring.
3. **In-kind contribution:** suitable materials, equipment, freight,
   warehousing, software or professional services accepted and controlled by
   the implementation partner.
4. **Professional contribution:** pro bono or reduced-cost engineering, WASH,
   architecture, safety, procurement, legal, audit or project-management work.
5. **Matched contribution:** a company agrees to match funds raised later by
   the authorized partner under an approved campaign.
6. **Introductions:** referral to a corporate foundation, supplier, donor,
   humanitarian organization or local implementation partner.

The phrase **pilot social-impact costs** means the documented costs required to
validate and deliver the humanitarian benefit. It does not mean a payment to
the project founder.

## Hasky Compensation Model

Hasky is a project identity, not a legal person or bank account. It cannot
receive donations, sign an employment agreement or issue an invoice by itself.
That does not mean the founder must work for free.

### Permanent separation of responsibilities

Hasky will not receive, hold, route, manage, approve or account for money
intended for construction, humanitarian delivery or beneficiary support. All
such funds must remain under the sole control of the approved fiscal host or
humanitarian implementation partner. That partner is responsible for banking,
procurement, contractor payments, financial controls, audit and donor
reporting.

Hasky's role is limited to concept development, technical coordination,
documentation, communications and transparent project reporting within the
scope approved by the partner. Hasky will not have access to the partner's bank
accounts, payment credentials or donation systems.

After a fiscal host or humanitarian partner accepts the project, the approved
budget may include reasonable compensation for genuine work such as:

- project direction and coordination;
- concept and technical development;
- documentation, reporting and transparency tools;
- partner, supplier and professional coordination; and
- approved travel or project expenses supported by receipts.

Compensation should follow one of three documented routes:

1. **Employment:** the host or partner employs the founder and handles payroll.
2. **Consulting contract:** a properly registered business invoices for defined
   deliverables when the relationship is genuinely independent.
3. **Occasional honorarium:** a limited payment is made to the individual and
   declared correctly as taxable income where this classification is legally
   appropriate.

The sponsor or partner must approve the role, rate, deliverables and payment
schedule. The founder must disclose the conflict and must not approve their own
compensation. Rates should be reasonable for the work and project budget.

The budget should show a clear line such as **Project coordination and technical
development**, instead of hiding compensation inside construction or family
support costs.

The following are prohibited:

- donations paid to a personal account or wallet;
- an undisclosed founder fee;
- compensation calculated as a percentage of donations raised;
- self-approved invoices or reimbursements;
- describing founder compensation as money delivered to beneficiaries; and
- assuming that unpaid work performed before an agreement will be reimbursed
  retroactively.

Any compensation for Hasky's professional work must be a separate, approved
project expense paid by the host or partner under a written employment,
consulting or honorarium arrangement. Receiving compensation for documented
work does not give Hasky custody or management authority over project funds.

## Short Proposal Letter

**Subject:** Private-sector partnership inquiry: HEARTBEAT Gaza shelter pilot

Dear [Name or Organization] team,

I am writing on behalf of HEARTBEAT Gaza, an independent early-stage concept
for a simple, durable and affordable family-home pilot for displaced families
in Gaza.

The proposed first phase is deliberately small: ten approximately 30 m2 family
homes, with weather protection, clean water, private sanitation, basic energy
and safe living space treated as essential infrastructure. The current designs
are concept studies, not approved construction drawings. No fundraising or
construction has begun.

HEARTBEAT is seeking established organizations and responsible private-sector
contributors willing to help evaluate the concept and support a responsible
path forward. Depending on your role, this could include technical review,
humanitarian implementation, fiscal sponsorship, a conditional funding pledge,
a restricted grant, donated materials or services, grant administration, or an
introduction to a more suitable partner.

Hasky would contribute the concept, open project documentation, design
coordination, technology and transparent reporting. Any humanitarian pilot
would need to be led by qualified organizations responsible for community
consultation, safeguarding, engineering, WASH, procurement, compliance, local
permissions and implementation.

Would your organization be open to a short introductory conversation, or could
you direct this inquiry to the appropriate team? I would be glad to provide a
two-page concept note and preliminary pilot assumptions for private review.

This inquiry does not claim a partnership or request an immediate transfer of
funds. We are asking whether your organization would consider a conversation
and, where appropriate, a conditional contribution toward defined pilot costs.
HEARTBEAT will not solicit or receive donations unless an authorized
organization has accepted responsibility for the legal, financial and
operational process.

Kind regards,

Hasky

## First Outreach List

### 1. Open Collective Europe Foundation

**Role requested:** European fiscal host and compliance partner.

**Eligibility position:** OCE's published application guide says initiatives
may be based anywhere in the world, while its fiscal-hosting FAQ describes OCE
as serving EU-based collectives. HEARTBEAT is coordinated from Denmark but
proposes humanitarian work in Gaza. Written confirmation of geographic
eligibility is therefore required before completing a full application.

**Humanitarian alignment:** The proposed public benefit is safe family shelter
with water, sanitation, basic energy and dignity for displaced families. This
is an alignment argument for OCE to assess, not a claim that the project has
already been accepted into its humanitarian portfolio.

**Tailored sentence:**

> We would like to learn whether HEARTBEAT may be eligible for humanitarian
> fiscal hosting while the concept is independently reviewed and an
> implementation partner is identified.

**Indicative timeline:**

- Months 0-2: hosting and implementation-partner eligibility.
- Months 2-4: technical, WASH and safeguarding validation.
- Months 4-6: local access, site, cost and feasibility review.
- Months 6-9: partner-led fundraising and procurement planning.
- Months 9-12: pilot only if approvals, access and funding are secured.

**Prospective funding sources:** Conditional private-sector pledges, restricted
foundation grants, in-kind materials and professional services, and later
public contributions through the approved host. No funding source is currently
committed, and none may be described as secured without written confirmation.

**Route:** Apply or contact through
https://opencollective.com/oce-foundation-eur

**Why it fits:** The Foundation states that it supports humanitarian and
philanthropic initiatives. Its published application guidance accepts
initiatives without their own legal entity and requires at least two named
administrators. Cryptocurrency contributions require separate prior approval
and are paid to hosted initiatives in fiat, not cryptocurrency.

## Additional Private-Sector Targets

These organizations should be approached after a fiscal host or humanitarian
partner has shown interest, because their published programmes generally expect
an NGO, nonprofit or established implementing partner.

### Grundfos Foundation

**Possible contribution:** Water-system design, pumps, treatment expertise,
equipment or restricted water and sanitation funding.

**Reason for fit:** The Foundation funds water and development work and partners
with established humanitarian organizations.

**Route:** https://www.pdjf.dk/en/

### Schneider Electric Foundation

**Possible contribution:** Safe energy-system design, equipment, technical
training, in-kind support or restricted energy-access funding.

**Reason for fit:** Its published criteria cover energy access, social
innovation and financial or in-kind support, but exclude individual projects.

**Route:** https://submityourproject.foundation.se.com/en

### Saint-Gobain Foundation

**Possible contribution:** Sustainable shelter materials, technical expertise
or restricted social-housing support.

**Reason for fit:** Its programme focuses on sustainable social housing, but a
project must be carried by a nonprofit and sponsored by a Saint-Gobain employee
or eligible retiree.

**Route:** https://fondation.saint-gobain-initiatives.com/en/

### A.P. Moller - Maersk

**Possible contribution:** Freight, containers, warehousing, customs and
humanitarian logistics expertise.

**Reason for fit:** Maersk documents in-kind logistics support delivered
through established humanitarian organizations. This makes an implementing
partner essential before making a logistics request.

**Route:** Use Maersk's official sustainability or humanitarian-partnership
channel after the implementation route is established.

### Construction and Technology Suppliers

Ask manufacturers and service companies for one defined contribution rather
than a vague sponsorship. Examples include insulation, roofing, doors and
windows, sanitary equipment, water storage, solar equipment, batteries,
project-management software, monitoring tools, independent testing or
professional hours. Every offer remains subject to the humanitarian partner's
technical standards, procurement rules and acceptance.

## Sending Rules

1. Send each inquiry privately and individually, not as a mass email.
2. Tailor the role requested; do not ask every recipient for the same thing.
3. Link to the public website, but do not attach beneficiary information.
4. Do not include bank details, cryptocurrency addresses or payment links.
5. Do not use recipient names or logos publicly without permission.
6. Record the date, recipient, channel, response and agreed follow-up.
7. Treat silence as no response, not endorsement.
8. Obtain independent advice before signing a binding or exclusive agreement.
