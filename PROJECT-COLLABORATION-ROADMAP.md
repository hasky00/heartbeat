# HEARTBEAT Gaza Collaboration Readiness Roadmap

## Purpose

This document turns HEARTBEAT Gaza from an early public concept into a project
that can be discussed responsibly with qualified humanitarian organizations.
Work will proceed one step at a time. Each gate must be completed before the
next gate begins.

## Current Status

- HEARTBEAT Gaza is an independent concept in the prototyping stage.
- Hasky is currently a GitHub identity/project, not a registered NGO, charity,
  humanitarian organization, contractor, or authorized representative of any
  organization operating in Gaza.
- The website must continue to state this status clearly.
- No organization currently endorses, funds, implements, or formally partners
  with HEARTBEAT unless a written agreement proves otherwise.
- The proposed homes and settlement images are concepts, not approved
  construction drawings.

## What Hasky Can Do Now

- Develop and document the concept.
- Publish non-misleading research and proposed designs.
- Request non-binding conversations and technical feedback.
- Seek qualified engineering, WASH, humanitarian, legal, and safeguarding
  advisers.
- Invite an established organization to evaluate or adopt the proposal.
- Prepare open, transparent budgets and project documentation.

## What Hasky Must Not Do Yet

- Claim to be an NGO, charity, humanitarian agency, or implementation partner.
- Claim that donations are charitable or tax-deductible.
- Collect money on behalf of families in Gaza without an authorized legal and
  financial structure.
- Add a public cryptocurrency wallet for humanitarian fundraising.
- Use another organization's name or logo in a way that implies endorsement.
- Promise that homes will be built, select beneficiaries, collect sensitive
  beneficiary information, purchase materials, or begin construction.
- Transfer funds or assets into Gaza without professional sanctions, AML/CFT,
  anti-diversion, and local legal review.

## Recommended Operating Model

Use a **partner-led model** for the first pilot.

Under this model:

- Hasky contributes the concept, public documentation, design coordination,
  technology, and transparent reporting tools.
- A registered humanitarian organization or fiscal sponsor receives and
  controls funds.
- Hasky never receives, holds, routes, manages, approves, or accounts for
  construction, humanitarian, or beneficiary funds and has no access to the
  partner's banking or donation systems.
- A qualified organization with Gaza access leads beneficiary protection,
  procurement, local permissions, logistics, safeguarding, and implementation.
- Licensed engineers and WASH professionals approve the final design.
- Cryptocurrency, if accepted at all, is processed by a regulated intermediary
  and converted or disbursed under the partner's compliance controls.

This is more realistic for a first pilot than immediately creating a new NGO.
Creating a legal nonprofit can be reconsidered after professional advice and a
credible partner assessment.

#### Gate 1 Decision Record

**Recorded:** 5 October 2026

HEARTBEAT will pursue a partner-led model. Hasky will remain the concept and
technology contributor and will not represent itself as an NGO, receive or
control humanitarian donations, manage construction funds, select
beneficiaries, or implement work in Gaza. A registered humanitarian partner
must own the operational programme, compliance process, funds, banking,
procurement, local agreements, and implementation decisions.

The public project identity is **HEARTBEAT Gaza, an independent concept
initiated by Hasky**. It currently has no separate legal entity or operating
jurisdiction. Hasky may speak about the concept and send non-binding inquiries,
but may not sign an agreement on behalf of an NGO or bind any humanitarian
organization. Any future pilot agreement must identify the registered partner,
governing law, authorized signatories, and division of responsibility.

Independent legal advice remains required before Hasky signs a binding
agreement, receives funds, creates a nonprofit entity, or begins regulated
activity. Until then, the partner-led decision is a planning position rather
than legal authorization.

The first organization to approach will be the International Rescue Committee
(IRC), because it has documented experience supporting shelters in Gaza through
the Palestinian organization Juzoor and has previously worked with ENS Labs and
Endaoment on compliant crypto-to-fiat fundraising for Gaza.

The initial approach will request:

1. confirmation of whether IRC considers unsolicited early-stage concepts;
2. referral to the appropriate shelter, innovation, or partnership team;
3. guidance on the minimum evidence and legal structure required; and
4. permission to send a short concept note for technical review.

It will not request money, claim a partnership, or propose placing a public
crypto wallet on the HEARTBEAT website.

### Gate 7: Begin Formal Outreach

Use `PARTNER-OUTREACH-PACK.md` for private, non-binding inquiries. Priority
organizations for the first round are:

1. Open Collective Europe Foundation for European humanitarian fiscal hosting.
2. Global Impact for international fiscal sponsorship and grant administration.
3. International Rescue Committee and its Gaza implementation partners for
   technical review and referral.
4. Mercy Corps and Mercy Corps Ventures for humanitarian and innovation review.
5. Save the Children for safeguarding and family-shelter review.
6. Affinity Partners for a private-sector social-impact channel, strategic

UNICEF State of Palestine and World Food Programme may be approached later for
standards or technical guidance if an established partner recommends the

The first request should be for technical assessment, eligibility guidance,
and the correct partnership channel. It should not claim endorsement or ask
the organization to transfer money immediately.

**Gate 7 completion:** Outreach has produced a documented response, referral,
